Tfchess is supposed to be chess program that supports roleplay based around transforming the opponnent.
-First it will focus on either local hot seat play or playing against chess engines of varying strenght
-It will need to be able to support player profiles, with ELO and notes
-It will need ability to stop timer on demand, so players can chat/roleplay

-It will have automatic intermissions. Every time a piece is captured or a kind is checked, timer will stop and a random image from player's images folder will show on the screen together with a message. Messages and images might be completely random or based on certain variables. Each player recieves image from their own folder (or folders - there might be different images based on whether the player took or lost the piece). Additionally, the image will show up for certain time, and a rhythmic pulsing will show up, something like in a rhythm game. Once the intermission ends, timer and game will resume.
